Linkora - Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Linkora is a live video and text chat app for adults only (18+). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page explains our standards against CSAE.

1. Adults only

  • Linkora is only for people aged 18 and above. Users must confirm they are 18+ before using the app.
  • The app is listed on Google Play with a target audience of 18 and over.
  • Anyone we find or suspect to be under 18 is removed and banned.

2. What is strictly prohibited

  • Any sexual content involving minors, or any sexualised talk about children
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation or trying to meet a minor
  • Sharing, showing or asking for CSAM in any form (video, image or text)
  • Nudity or sexual behavior on camera

3. In-app reporting and blocking

  • Every chat has a flag button. Users can report another user at any time and choose a reason, including child safety concerns.
  • A reported user is blocked from matching with the reporter again.
  • Devices that get repeated reports are banned.

4. How we act on reports

  • We review child safety reports as a priority and ban the device involved.
  • Linkora does not record or store video, audio or chat. If a report involves apparent CSAE, we preserve the report details we hold (reason and anonymous device ID) and share them with the relevant authorities.
  • We report apparent CSAE and CSAM to the relevant national and regional authorities, such as the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) in India and NCMEC in the United States, and we cooperate with law enforcement.
  • We follow all applicable child safety laws.
