Linkora - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Linkora ("we", "the app") lets adults (18+) meet random people through live video and text chat. This policy explains what data the app uses.

1. Who can use Linkora

Linkora is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly allow children under 18. If you believe a minor is using the app, report the user inside the app or write to us.

2. Data we collect and why

  • Gender and match preference (male/female, who you want to meet): sent to our server only to match you with another user. Your own gender is also stored on your phone.
  • Anonymous device ID (a random ID created by the app): used for safety - blocking users and banning devices that are reported repeatedly.
  • Chat messages: passed through our server to your chat partner in real time. We do not store them.
  • Reports: when you report a user we log the reason and the reported device ID to enforce our rules.
  • IP address: visible to your chat partner and to STUN servers (Google) that help connect video calls, which is how peer-to-peer video works.

3. Camera and microphone

The app asks for camera and microphone permission only to run live chats. Video and audio travel directly between you and your partner (WebRTC). We do not record, store or analyse your video or audio. Your partner may be able to record their own screen; do not share anything you do not want to be seen.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not track your precise location.

5. Advertising

The app may show ads from Google AdMob in future versions. If so, Google may use an advertising ID as described in Google's Privacy Policy, and this policy and the Play Store Data safety form will be updated first.

6. Safety and moderation

You can report and block any user with the flag button. Reported devices can be banned. See our Terms and Community Guidelines.

7. Data retention and deletion

Chat messages, video and audio are not stored. Reports and ban records are kept only as long as needed for safety. Data stored on your phone (gender, device ID) is removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the app. To ask us to delete safety records linked to your device ID, contact us.

8. Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and strangers you meet can be anyone - never share personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
