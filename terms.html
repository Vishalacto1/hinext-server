Linkora - Terms of Use and Community Guidelines

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Terms of Use

  • You must be at least 18 years old to use Linkora.
  • Linkora is provided "as is". You use it at your own risk and are responsible for what you show and say.
  • We can suspend or ban any device that breaks these terms, without notice.
  • Linkora cannot verify who users are or the gender they choose.
  • Our Privacy Policy forms part of these terms.

Community Guidelines

Not allowed
  • Nudity, sexual content or sexual behavior on camera
  • Anyone under 18
  • Harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats or violence
  • Self-harm content or encouraging it
  • Illegal activity, scams, spam or advertising
  • Recording or sharing another person's video or chat without consent
  • Sharing personal information of yourself or others

Reporting and enforcement

Tap the flag button during a chat, choose a reason, and the user is reported and blocked from matching with you again. Repeated reports lead to a temporary or permanent ban of the device. You can leave any chat at any time with Stop or Next.

Safety tips

Do not share your real name, phone number, address, school, workplace, passwords or money. If something makes you feel unsafe, leave the chat and report the user. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services.
